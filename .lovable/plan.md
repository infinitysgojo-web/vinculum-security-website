# Dashboard Mathematical Interface and End-to-End Integrity

## Goal
Make the dashboard visibly match the system that has been described: analysts must be able to enter precise decimal readings, see the governing mathematics and thresholds, understand what each desk sends downstream, and follow the mission chain without relying on an external manual. Restore the promised case-score reference directly beneath the public atlas. The master PDF remains out of scope.

## Dashboard interface

- Replace the four whole-number-only diagnostic sliders with paired slider and numeric-entry controls accepting values from 1.0 to 5.0 in 0.1 increments, including direct entry such as 3.1.
- Apply the same precision to the Cross-Case Stress Tester and make its descriptive labels work safely with decimals.
- Add a restrained mathematical reading beside Diagnostics showing the state vector, mean, limiting factor, spread/variance, band, and nearest-case distance/resonance in plain language and notation.
- Add a compact mission-chain panel above each desk showing which upstream readings are available, which stage produced them, what the current desk consumes, and what it will publish next.
- Make active synchronization, independent override, locks, released conditions, and required next actions visually unmistakable without overcrowding mobile screens.

## Mathematical and state integrity

- Harmonize the canonical bands throughout the live dashboard: failing at 1.0–2.0, fragile above 2.0 through 3.0, viable above 3.0 through 4.0, fortified above 4.0 through 5.0.
- Standardize trend language to Improving, Static, and Deteriorating, with multipliers 0.85, 1.00, and 1.15.
- Make Track Sequencing use the communal-bond tolerance rule: no Track I lead when communal is failing, one phase when fragile, two phases when viable or fortified.
- Correct readiness logic so labels say whether a condition is satisfied or still locked, add the missing independent-verifier lock for elevated rupture, and ensure lifecycle results are stage-specific rather than copied across every stage.
- Preserve the deterministic, browser-local MissionEngine architecture and zero-AI rule.

## Case Studies page

- Move the existing 32-score cross-case matrix from below the cards to directly beneath the Equal Earth atlas, where it was originally promised.
- Make every row open its full case study, retain the explanatory pattern text, and provide a stacked mobile presentation rather than forcing a wide table.
- Keep the Stress Tester private in Dashboard Diagnostics; the public page remains scholarly rather than operational.
