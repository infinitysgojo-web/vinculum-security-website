# Project Architecture

- Seal formatting follows exact comparison; PDF hashes use visible chunks; pouch ticks confirm requests only. Why: readability must preserve evidence and browser limits.

- PDF exports reserve body space for bounded running headers and footers; statutory notices belong in full-width notice blocks, not compact mastheads. Why: long legal text must never collide with branding or page numbering.

- Keep scholarly working tools embedded in their relevant existing page, with browser-local state only, to preserve a focused reading architecture without new routes or data storage.
- Dashboard desks share readings only through the MissionEngine signal bus (src/components/desk/engine.ts: usePublish/useEngine, SyncPanel override); why: one deterministic, browser-local chain from assessment to handover.
- Enforce framing restrictions through response-header CSP with self and trusted Lovable ancestors, never parent-navigation scripts or hide-on-error fallbacks; why: sandboxed editor previews must render while unrelated framing remains blocked.
- The Chancellery Sentinel (src/components/desk/ChancellerySentinel.tsx) owns the dashboard's only inactivity timer, the panic keys and the DOM canary, and never navigates; Dashboard.tsx decides what idle and panic do. Why: two idle timers race, and only Dashboard.tsx and Login.tsx may reference /dashboard or /login.
- Free text is sanitized on paste and on blur (src/lib/sanitizeFields.ts), never per keystroke, with NFC rather than NFKC; verifiers report hidden characters but hash the record as received. Why: typing and caret must not break, legal text must not change meaning, and a verifier must not mask tampering.
- The dashboard runs two mandates over the same 18 desks: a Mission Foyer, then Preset Proving Ground or Custom Operational Mission (src/components/mode/). A versioned browser-local workspace wraps the saved-session desk payload (profile, binder, bus signals, position) with tray entries, decisions, lifecycle and lineage; it is sealed into the enclave when one is configured, otherwise kept in localStorage, and unreadable records are quarantined, never erased. The mode shell reads the bus but never publishes to it or keeps desk state of its own; why: one signal chain, one set of formulas, no parallel store, and no new route.
- The 13-point Chancellery operational suite lives on three surfaces inside /dashboard, with no new route: Surface 1 in the Chancellery Wire (Signal Inspector reads the bus through busMonitor.ts and never publishes; the Decision Log is a view of ledgerStore; the Handover Seal hashes canonical JSON with SubtleCrypto and keeps it in sessionStorage), Surface 2 in mode/RehearsalDrawer.tsx (posture branches in sessionStorage `vs:branches`, re-hydrating the bus the way opening a workspace does, while the stored workspace keeps the baseline posture), Surface 3 in the shell (one hotkey listener in useSovereignHotkeys, which never stops propagation so the Sentinel still counts Escape). Why: every new reading is a view of the same bus and formulas, so the suite can never disagree with the desks.
- Timeline scrubbers and [ƒx] proofs call the desks' own functions (compoundStress, ruptureIndex, decayHorizon, forumFitness, escrowWaterfall); never re-implement a formula in a view. Why: the final step of a timeline and every proof must equal what the desk displays.
- The dashboard header is three bands and nothing more: the Sentinel strip (36 px, pinned; reads the Sentinel through useSentinel and never owns a timer), the Mission Operations Deck (content-sized identity and wrapping tool groups; titles remain readable and all tools stay reachable; Vault, Ledger and Controls open as panels beneath it, closed by default), and the desk band (fixed h-12, pinned under the strip, divisions and desk stepper in one horizontal scroller). Why: toolbar overflow must never hide controls, and switching divisions never moves the page.
- Under the privacy mask, narrative fields and content selects freeze; navigation selects (desk picker, theatre preset, Mission Spine) carry data-shroud-nav, stay usable, and mask their own option labels. Why: option text is not reached by the text-layer pass, and a presenter must still be able to navigate.
- Questioning desks interpret nonblank custom-script lines as ordered questions and read counterpart tactics from MissionEngine customNotes; seating is limited only by the available roster. Why: no silent truncation, duplicate tactic store or arbitrary delegation ceiling.
- Counterpart stall handling uses operator-selected conditions mapped to existing default follow-up questions and per-seat state; it never infers answers, advances phases, publishes findings or clears gates. Why: deterministic source reuse is guidance, not evidence verification.
- Offline sign-in verifies only a credential enrolled on the device by a real online sign-in; never ship credential material (emails, salts, digests) in the bundle. Why: the bundle is public and invites offline cracking.
- Desk presentation uses scoped diplomatic-workspace styles and container-aware instrument-layout/instrument-pair grids; dense What-If, Escrow, Forum, Decay and Timeline instruments stay full-width, while inputs, gauges and rooting tests form separate balanced tiers with content allowed to grow. Why: readable text and continuous working space take priority over fixed shapes or tall mismatched columns without changing the signal chain.
- In Custom Operational Missions the bus drops a desk's publications until the operator first works that desk (input, change or control click inside its surface); shell publishers (mission, preset) are exempt. Why: a desk's starting values are not an assessment.
- The Dominum Index (dominumIndex in engine.ts) is a derived view of bus readings only: shown on Mission Readiness with its [ƒx] proof and carried to handover through telemetryLines; it never publishes a signal. Why: the Dominum Effect must agree with the desks it summarises.
- The Governing Relational Laws console lives in the TelemetryStrip (governingLaws in TelemetryStrip.tsx) and only reads the bus and engine functions; it asserts a breach/holding status only where the engine defines a threshold, otherwise shows the live reading. Why: the laws must stay prominent without inventing values or diverging from the desks.
- Public header navigation groups the dashboard overview under Practice and Commentary/Glossary under Reference, without duplicate footer entries. Why: public reading destinations stay accessible through one consistent navigation on desktop and mobile.
- Public navigation structured data reuses PUBLIC_PILLARS and navigationLd from seo-entities.ts; its static index.html counterpart must match the same entity ID, paths and order. Why: crawlers and rendered pages need consistent directory signals without adding routes.
- Bus change keys preserve explicit undefined clears and array order while sorting object keys; canonical seal JSON is unchanged. Why: clears reach the schema guard and key order cannot manufacture publications.
- Browser-local workspace writes are serialized and tracked by latest revision, with unload warnings only while dirty; exact quarantine duplicates are coalesced without trimming distinct records. Why: stale writes cannot mark newer edits saved or multiply recovery records.
- Sentinel activity checks elapsed wall time before renewal; visibility and focus check expiry and share one clipboard eviction state machine. Why: suspended timers cannot renew expired security deadlines.
- Counterpart exports carry every seated script and its operator checkpoint; asymmetry proofs use normalized Euclidean distance and never invented party coordinates. Why: exports preserve actual inputs and proofs match their instruments.
- Desk orientation reuses DESK_GUIDES outside desk surfaces, navigates through Dashboard and stores only a non-sensitive invitation preference; it never publishes or engages a desk. Why: learning must not become an assessment or bypass the Sentinel and privacy safeguards.
